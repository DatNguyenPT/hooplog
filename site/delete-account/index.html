HoopLog

Delete your account

You can delete your HoopLog account yourself, in the app. It takes about a minute and it cannot be undone.

How to delete it

  1. Open HoopLog on your phone and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account, and sign in if you are not signed in.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address and your sign-in.
  • Anything stored online under your account.

The account is removed straight away and cannot be brought back.

What is not deleted

Your workouts, shooting sessions and plans are saved on your phone, not online, so deleting the account does not touch them. To erase them too, open Settings, then Your data, and delete your data there.
